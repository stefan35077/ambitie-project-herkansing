# User Test 

## Test Info
- **Test type:** Uninstructed user playtest  
- **Player:** My mom
- **Observer:** Stefan  
- **Duration:** 10 minutes  

## Goal
Check if a new player can understand and play the game without any explanation.

## Test Setup
- No instructions are given  
- Player starts the game and plays freely  

## Observations
 #### - Does the player understand the goal?
  - The player understood she had to click one of the level buttons to start the game, it did take a little bit of time.
 #### - Does the player discover how to shoot?
  - After she clicked some buttons she figured out she can shoot with the mouse button.
 #### - Does the player understand matching colors?
  - She understood she had to match the colors after a couple of shots.
 #### - Are power-up balls recognized as special?
   - Yes she realised the power up balls were different from other balls
 #### - Does the player get stuck or confused?
   - Yes in the beggining she got confused and did not really know how it worked but after a minure or 2 she figured it out and played trough the game with no issue.

## Player Feedback (after playing)
- What do you think the goal of the game is?
   - I think the goal is to match all balls on the board as fast as possible.
- Was anything unclear?
   - The game was clear and I understood what to do, it did take me a couple tries before I knew how to shoot the ball.
- Did you understand the power-ups?
   - I did not know what the power-ups did when I got them but I understood after using them a couple of times.
- What did you like or dislike?
   - I like the game and I like the drawings but the balls and the board look a bit boring and ugly.

## Result
- **Issues found:**  
None

## Conclusion
The game is overall pretty clear but the main issue is the visuals during gameplay.
